How to read the score ?

Think of `Entry Score` as a quick checklist. Higher scores mean the volatility backdrop and the option itself both look cleaner.

What makes a setup good ?

Better setups usually have lower extrinsic cost, tighter spreads, decent volume/open interest, and a curve that is not screaming front-end stress.

What makes a setup dangerous ?

Be careful when spreads are wide, liquidity is thin, or the curve is in backwardation. That usually means tougher fills and a shakier short-vol regime.
